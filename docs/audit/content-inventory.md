## Observations relevant to the topic proposal

**1. Locale parity is complete.** All 30 topics exist in both locales. The ja↔en mapping is
by meaning, not by slug (`maindfullness-to-nou` ↔ `eight-weeks-that-change-the-brain`), so
nothing here can be found by slug comparison alone.

**2. Cluster density is uneven.** Counted by pair:

| Cluster | Pairs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion & social brain (anger, anxiety, gratitude, loneliness, empathy) | 5 | Densest cluster |
| Contemplative practice (zen, mindfulness, ma, totonou) | 4 | Well covered |
| Sleep (memory, deprivation/amygdala, glymphatic) | 3 | Night side only — no circadian/morning entry |
| Cognition & decision (intuition, law of attraction, procrastination) | 3 | No decision-cost article |
| ADHD | 2 | Saturated; avoid a third |
| Stress & recovery (burnout, vacation) | 2 | — |
| Audio (music, binaural beats) | 2 | — |
| Dopamine baseline (focus, phone, post-holiday, hare/ke, lotus sutra) | 5 | Foundational; wanting-vs-liking still missing |
| Body inputs (exercise, breathing, nature walking) | 3 | **No nutrition, no light, no caffeine** |
| AI anxiety | 1 | Thin, but niche |

**3. Nothing at all covers what the body takes in.** There are three "what the body does"
articles (exercise, breathing, walking) and zero "what the body ingests or receives"
articles — no caffeine, no food/gut, no light exposure. For a dopamine-first publication
these are the highest-volume adjacent keywords still unclaimed.

**4. Internal linking is nearly absent.** Only four EN files and four JA files contain any
inline `/posts/…` link, all inside the two `series.ts` clusters
(`focus-dopamine`, `japanese-philosophy`). 26 of 30 pairs are orphans with no editorial
inbound link. New articles should be written with explicit link targets from the start, and
the proposal below names them per topic.

**5. `series.ts` covers 3 of 30 topics.** `japanese-philosophy` lists only `totonou`, though
at least eight articles qualify. That is a separate finding (site/config, not content) and is
not addressed by the new-article proposal.
