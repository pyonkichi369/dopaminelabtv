# Proposed New Articles — 6 topics × 2 locales = 12 articles

Derived from [`content-inventory.md`](./content-inventory.md) (60 existing articles, audited
2026-07-26). Each topic ships as an EN + JA set per the house cadence, and each obeys the
content formula: **dopamine is the subject, neuroscience leads, Japanese philosophy
complements** (see [`style-checklist.md`](./style-checklist.md)).

Selection rule applied: a topic qualifies only if its primary keyword is not already the
primary keyword of any existing article, and it strengthens a thin cluster rather than
splitting traffic with a dense one. Two otherwise attractive topics were **rejected** for
cannibalization: *cold plunge / sauna dopamine* (collides with `totonou-no-kagaku` /
`totonou-what-your-brain-needs`) and *multitasking & task switching* (collides with
`naze-shuchu-dekinai` / `why-you-cant-focus-anymore`).

Titles below are working titles. No article body has been drafted.

## 1. Caffeine removes a brake rather than adding energy

- **Working title (ja):** カフェインは、エネルギーを足していない
- **Working title (en):** Caffeine Doesn't Add Energy — It Removes a Brake
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「カフェイン ドーパミン」 / en "caffeine and dopamine"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** Caffeine works by blocking adenosine receptors, which
  disinhibits dopamine signalling and defers rather than repays sleep pressure — a mechanism
  no existing article touches, despite three sleep articles and a whole focus cluster that
  readers reach while holding a coffee. Japanese complement: 一服 — the tea pause was always
  a pause, not a fuel stop.
- **Internal links to / from:** `sleep-makes-memories` / `suimin-ga-kioku-wo-tsukuru` (the
  sleep debt caffeine hides) and `why-you-cant-focus-anymore` / `naze-shuchu-dekinai` (the
  baseline it is being used to patch).

## 2. Sugar reaches the reward system before it reaches the body

- **Working title (ja):** 甘いものがやめられないのは、味覚の話ではない
- **Working title (en):** Sugar Reaches the Reward System Before It Reaches Your Body
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「砂糖 ドーパミン」 / en "sugar dopamine reward"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** There is no nutrition article at all in 60 files — the site
  covers what the body *does* (exercise, breathing, walking) but nothing it *takes in*, even
  though post-ingestive nutrient signalling drives striatal dopamine independently of taste.
  Japanese complement: 腹八分目 — a stopping rule that predates the mechanism it exploits.
- **Internal links to / from:** `why-you-cant-put-down-your-phone` /
  `sumatho-wo-yamerarenai-honto-no-riyu` (same reward-prediction loop, different input) and
  `hare-and-ke-dopamine-reset` / `hare-to-ke-dopamine-reset` (hedonic adaptation).

## 3. Morning light sets the clock the whole day runs on

- **Working title (ja):** 朝の光が、その日の夜を決めている
- **Working title (en):** Morning Light Sets the Clock Your Whole Day Runs On
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「朝の光 体内時計」 / en "morning light circadian rhythm"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** All three sleep articles describe the night — consolidation,
  amygdala reactivity, glymphatic clearance — and none describes the input that schedules it,
  namely retinal ganglion cell entrainment of the suprachiasmatic nucleus and the resulting
  cortisol awakening response. Japanese complement: 朝の勤行 — the practice traditions all
  start at first light, and the placement is not incidental.
- **Internal links to / from:** `sleep-loss-makes-you-reactive` /
  `nemuranai-to-okorippoku-naru` (the cost of a mistimed clock) and
  `the-brain-washes-itself-but-not-with-sound` / `nou-no-gomi-wa-oto-dewa-nagasenai` (the
  night-side process light schedules).

## 4. A habit is a behaviour that stopped needing willpower

- **Working title (ja):** 習慣になった行動は、なぜ意志を使わないのか
- **Working title (en):** Why a Habit Stops Needing Willpower
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「習慣化 脳科学」 / en "habit formation neuroscience"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** The existing pair covers habit only in its failure modes —
  procrastination as design failure, anxiety as an unwanted loop — and never the constructive
  mechanism, the transfer of control from goal-directed ventral striatum to habitual
  dorsolateral striatum that makes repetition cheap. Japanese complement: 型 and 稽古 — form
  practised until it stops costing attention is the same claim, stated eight centuries early.
- **Internal links to / from:** `procrastination-is-not-a-willpower-problem` /
  `saki-nobashi-no-noukagaku` (the starting-cost side) and `why-anxiety-becomes-a-habit` /
  `fuan-ga-kuse-ni-naru-riyu` (the same mechanism running unwanted).

## 5. Wanting and liking run on two different systems

- **Working title (ja):** 「欲しい」と「好き」は、別の回路で動いている
- **Working title (en):** Wanting and Liking Are Two Different Systems
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「ドーパミン 欲しい 好き 違い」 / en "wanting vs liking dopamine"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** This is the foundational distinction of the whole dopamine
  literature — incentive salience is dissociable from hedonic pleasure — and five articles
  lean on it implicitly while none states it, which leaves the site's central concept
  unexplained to a first-time reader arriving from search. Japanese complement: 知足 — the
  practice of noticing that wanting more and enjoying more are not the same quantity.
- **Internal links to / from:** `hare-and-ke-dopamine-reset` / `hare-to-ke-dopamine-reset`
  (what a reset actually resets) and `what-the-lotus-sutra-knew-2500-years-ago` /
  `hokekyo-ga-2500nen-mae-ni-shitte-ita-koto` (craving read as prediction error).

## 6. Small decisions are what wear you out

- **Working title (ja):** 小さな決断が、なぜいちばん疲れるのか
- **Working title (en):** Why Small Decisions Are the Ones That Wear You Out
- **Target primary keyword:** ja「決断疲れ 脳」 / en "decision fatigue brain"
- **Angle / why it's a gap:** Decision fatigue is a high-volume query with a real mechanism —
  effort-cost accounting in anterior cingulate and prefrontal cortex, where the cost is
  charged per decision rather than per decision's importance — and nothing in the cognition
  cluster covers the price of choosing, only the price of delaying. Japanese complement:
  一汁一菜 — a fixed form that removes the daily choice rather than optimising it.
- **Internal links to / from:** `what-burnout-does-to-your-brain` /
  `naze-yakitsukita-ato-yaruki-ga-modora-nai-no-ka` (where accumulated cost lands) and
  `why-vacation-doesnt-restore-your-brain` / `kyuka-ga-nou-wo-kaifuku-shinai-riyu` (why time
  off doesn't clear it).
